## Bloom Queen

“Some women just need to use their talent and bloom.”

There are women who are not fighting demons.
They’re fighting fear.
They’re fighting comfort zones.
They’re fighting self-doubt.
They’re fighting the small version of themselves.

The Bloom Queen is for the woman who:

- wants to start a business
- wants to step into her gift
- wants to stop hiding
- wants to stop shrinking
- wants to stop playing small
- wants to flourish
- wants to become visible
- wants to become HER

She is not fighting for survival.
She is fighting for expansion.

That is a queen too.
