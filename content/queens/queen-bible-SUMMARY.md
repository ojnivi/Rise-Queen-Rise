# Queen Bible — Quick Map

Public summary only. Full playbook is held privately (not in this repo).
Uploaded direction: Juanivi, 2026-09-29. Profiles expanded same day with approved emotional-arc additions. Updated 2026-10-01: Queen of Worth added as foundation profile; every queen now has a short profile card.

## 5 Content Pillars
1. **Self-Worth** — reclaiming inherent value (foundation)
2. **Boundaries** — naming, setting, and holding limits
3. **Identity** — who she is beneath the roles
4. **Grief & Release** — honoring loss, letting go
5. **Power & Presence** — voice, space, sovereign energy

Worth is the foundation. The butterfly is catalyst and symbol — not a queen name. Brand rule for new video direction: no wings-on-queen; cards stay visual-agnostic.

## Visual identity
Shared brand DNA (purple–gold luxury, Lady Butterfly Designs watermark optional footnote). **Different look per queen** — not one identical face across the roster. Each profile is an emotional season women identify with, not a mythical character.

## Queen Profiles (emotional states — not mythical names)
Women identify with a season, not a fantasy character. Warm, fierce, ministry/empowerment voice for Black and Afro-American women.

### The Worth Queen (Queen of Worth) — foundation face
- **Who she is:** The woman who finally knows she was enough before she earned a thing.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Inherent value, dignity, and self-respect — she will not bargain her worth for approval, crumbs, or silence.
- **Risen from:** Years of proving, shrinking, and waiting for permission to matter.

### From Queen Bible

#### The Exhausted Warrior Queen
- **Who she is:** Strong for everyone else until her body and spirit say stop.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Rest as power — she will not glorify burnout or call collapse “hustle.”
- **Risen from:** Carrying the load alone while calling it strength.

#### The Invisible Queen
- **Who she is:** Present in every room and somehow still unseen.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Being witnessed and named — she will not accept being background noise in her own life.
- **Risen from:** Shrinking so others could take up space.

#### The Heartbroken Queen
- **Who she is:** Loving deeply after love has cost her.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Grief that is honest — she will not pretend betrayal or loss didn’t happen.
- **Risen from:** A broken trust, a emptied bed, a love that left marks.

#### The Comparison Queen
- **Who she is:** Measuring her timeline against every highlight reel she scrolls.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Her own pace and path — she will not trade her lane for someone else’s highlight.
- **Risen from:** Scrolling herself into scarcity and shame.

#### The Almost-There Queen
- **Who she is:** One step from the breakthrough and terrified of the last leap.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Finishing what she started — she will not live forever in “almost.”
- **Risen from:** Near-misses, delayed doors, and fear of actually arriving.

#### The People-Pleasing Queen
- **Who she is:** Fluent in everyone else’s comfort except her own.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Honest yes and sacred no — she will not abandon herself to keep the peace.
- **Risen from:** A lifetime of “sure,” “it’s fine,” and swallowing her truth.

#### The Overthinking Queen
- **Who she is:** Replaying every word, every look, every what-if until she’s stuck.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Clarity over spirals — she will not let analysis steal her next move.
- **Risen from:** Anxiety dressed up as responsibility.

#### The Numb Queen
- **Who she is:** Surviving by feeling less so the ache can’t reach her.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Feeling again without apology — she will not confuse numbness with peace.
- **Risen from:** Pain that taught her to shut the lights off inside.

#### The Angry Queen
- **Who she is:** Rage that finally found a voice after being told to stay sweet.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Holy anger as signal — she will not shrink her fire to make others comfortable.
- **Risen from:** Silenced frustration, injustice, and “be nice” that cost her self.

#### The Starting-Over Queen
- **Who she is:** Beginning again with less baggage and more truth.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Clean slate without shame — she will not let a past chapter define her crown.
- **Risen from:** Endings, resets, and the courage to rebuild from scratch.

#### End-state: The Crowned
- **Who she is:** The woman who rose through every season and wears her story with sovereignty.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Integrated power — she will not hide her scars or her shine.
- **Risen from:** The full arc — silence through storm to presence.

### Added for emotional arc (approved 2026-09-29)

#### The Waiting Queen
- **Who she is:** Stuck between seasons; she knows something’s next but hasn’t moved yet.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Faithful patience without self-abandonment — she will not confuse delay with destiny forever.
- **Risen from:** Holding patterns, closed doors, and “not yet.”

#### The Rebuilding Queen
- **Who she is:** Putting her life back together piece by piece after the storm.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Slow, steady restoration — she will not rush healing to look “over it.”
- **Risen from:** Collapse, loss, or a life that fell apart.

#### The Boundary Queen
- **Who she is:** Learning to say no and choose herself (guilt included).
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Limits that protect her peace — she will not call self-erasure kindness.
- **Risen from:** Over-giving, intrusion, and relationships that had no walls.

#### The Soft-Power Queen
- **Who she is:** Healing quietly; strength without noise.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Gentle authority — she will not equate loud with powerful.
- **Risen from:** Forced hardness and the myth that soft means weak.

#### The Legacy Queen
- **Who she is:** Rising for her daughters, her circle, the women coming after her.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Generational freedom — she will not pass down the silence she survived.
- **Risen from:** Cycles she refused to hand to the next generation.

#### The Returning Queen
- **Who she is:** Left herself for everyone else and is finding her way home.
- **Stands for / would not stand for:** Coming back to her own name — she will not stay lost in other people’s needs.
- **Risen from:** Self-abandonment dressed as duty and love.

## Rising Order (7 levels)
The Silence → The Crack → The Storm → The Clearing → The Rebuild → The Rise → The Crowned
