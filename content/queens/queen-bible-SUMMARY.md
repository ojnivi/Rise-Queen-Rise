# Queen Bible — Quick Map

Public summary only. Full playbook is held privately (not in this repo).
Uploaded direction: Juanivi, 2026-09-29. Profiles expanded same day with approved emotional-arc additions.

## 5 Content Pillars
1. **Self-Worth** — reclaiming inherent value (foundation)
2. **Boundaries** — naming, setting, and holding limits
3. **Identity** — who she is beneath the roles
4. **Grief & Release** — honoring loss, letting go
5. **Power & Presence** — voice, space, sovereign energy

### Added for emotional arc (approved 2026-09-29)
- **The Waiting Queen** — stuck between seasons; she knows something’s next but hasn’t moved yet
- **The Rebuilding Queen** — putting her life back together piece by piece after the storm
- **The Boundary Queen** — learning to say no and choose herself (guilt included)
- **The Soft-Power Queen** — healing quietly; strength without noise
- **The Legacy Queen** — rising for her daughters, her circle, the women coming after her
- **The Returning Queen** — left herself for everyone else and is finding her way home

## Rising Order (7 levels)
