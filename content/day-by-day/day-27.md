# Day 27 — THE ROYAL FLIGHT
**Week:** Week 4 — Crown & Throne

## Graphic Prompt
The crowned African American butterfly queen flies above a golden city at sunset, wings fully extended, purple and gold butterflies trailing behind her like a comet tail. Her gown floats dramatically, layered with silk, gold crystals, and butterfly-shaped couture panels. She looks powerful, peaceful, and free. Epic fantasy, luxury fashion, triumphant ascension.

## Voiceover
“Freedom looks different when you fought for it. It feels like breath. It feels like sky. It feels like finally becoming too vast for the limits that once held you.”

## CTA
Comment “I AM FREE.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
