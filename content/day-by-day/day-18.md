# Day 18 — THE GARDEN OF NEW POWER
**Week:** Week 3 — Breakthrough

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman walks through an enchanted royal garden where every flower is shaped like a butterfly. She wears a purple and gold floral couture masterpiece with oversized butterfly wings trailing behind her like a cape. Purple and gold butterflies land on her hair, forming a living tiara. Soft gold light, abundance, rebirth, divine feminine luxury.

## Voiceover
“When you heal, you bloom differently. Your softness returns. Your joy returns. Your power no longer has to prove itself. It simply enters the room.”

## CTA
Comment “I AM BLOOMING.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
