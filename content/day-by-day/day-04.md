# Day 4 — LETTING GO OF WHAT HELD HER DOWN
**Week:** Week 1 — Call & Cocoon

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman stands in a dark royal-purple room releasing heavy gold chains from her wrists. As the chains fall, they transform into purple and gold butterflies. She wears a luxurious purple velvet haute couture gown with gold crystal embroidery and sculptural shoulders. Her face shows peace and strength. Behind her, faint butterfly wings begin to glow. Elegant, dramatic, editorial, regal transformation energy.

## Voiceover
“Not everything you carried was yours. Some weight was handed to you by fear, pain, and expectation. Today, you release what was never meant to follow you into your next level.”

## CTA
What are you releasing today? Comment one word.

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
