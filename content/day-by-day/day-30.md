# Day 30 — RISE QUEEN RISE
**Week:** Week 4 — Crown & Throne

## Graphic Prompt
Final transformation scene. The crowned African American butterfly queen rises from her golden throne into a powerful column of purple and gold light. Her wings spread impossibly wide, jeweled, luxurious, and radiant. Her couture gown flows like a royal nebula made of purple silk, molten gold, crystals, and butterfly-shaped panels. Thousands of purple and gold butterflies spiral around her, forming a giant crown in the sky above her. Ultimate ascension, cinematic finale, luxury mythic fashion, victorious becoming, powerful and emotional.

## Voiceover
“Look at her now. She survived what was meant to break her. She released what was meant to hold her. She rose from what was meant to bury her. She is no longer waiting. She is no longer becoming in secret. She is crowned. She is winged. She is risen. Rise Queen Rise.”

## CTA
Comment “I HAVE ARRIVED.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
