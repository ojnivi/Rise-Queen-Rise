# Day 25 — QUEEN OF HER OWN BECOMING
**Week:** Week 4 — Crown & Throne

## Graphic Prompt
An African American butterfly queen stands in a palace made of crystal, butterflies, and gold light. Her wings are fully grown, enormous, and luxurious with purple velvet texture and gold filigree veins. Her gown is royal, architectural, and radiant. She gently holds one purple and gold butterfly in her palm. Sovereign elegance, divine transformation, couture fantasy.

## Voiceover
“She became the queen of her own becoming. Not because no one doubted her. Not because the path was easy. But because she chose herself until the crown had no choice but to stay.”

## CTA
Comment “I CHOOSE ME.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
