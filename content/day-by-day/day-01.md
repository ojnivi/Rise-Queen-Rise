# Day 1 — THE CALL
**Week:** Week 1 — Call & Cocoon

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman stands in a glowing golden doorway wearing a simple deep-purple dress, looking hesitant but drawn toward the light. Purple and gold butterflies gather at her feet, with one luminous butterfly landing over her heart. In the distance, a jeweled crown floats in soft golden light, calling her forward. The scene should feel like the first moment of awakening, cinematic, emotional, luxurious, royal purple and metallic gold color palette, soft mist, glowing skin, high-fashion transformation fantasy.

## Voiceover
“There comes a moment when the queen inside you starts whispering louder than your fear. You may have forgotten who you are, but your spirit remembers. You were never ordinary. You were only waiting for the moment to rise.”

## CTA
Comment “I REMEMBER” if you’re ready to answer the call.

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
