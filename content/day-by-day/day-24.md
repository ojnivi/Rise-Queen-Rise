# Day 24 — THE CROWN DESCENDS
**Week:** Week 4 — Crown & Throne

## Graphic Prompt
A golden crown finally touches the head of an African American woman as purple and gold butterflies form a complete circle around her. Her eyes are closed in peaceful acceptance. Her full butterfly wings shimmer behind her. She wears a royal purple haute couture gown with gold gemstone embroidery and a cathedral train. Sacred coronation, luminous luxury, emotional acceptance.

## Voiceover
“This is the moment you stop asking if you are enough. This is the moment you stop shrinking to be accepted. The crown has descended because you finally made room for it.”

## CTA
Drop 👑 if you’re accepting your crown.

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
