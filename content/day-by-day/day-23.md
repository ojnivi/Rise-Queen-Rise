# Day 23 — NO PERMISSION NEEDED
**Week:** Week 4 — Crown & Throne

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman stands before a closed golden palace gate and pushes it open with one hand. Purple and gold butterflies surge through the gate like a wave. She wears a bold avant-garde purple gown with gold sculptural shoulders, full wings, and a radiant crown beginning to appear. Commanding, fearless, luxurious empowerment.

## Voiceover
“You do not need permission to become who you were created to be. You do not need approval to rise. The gate opens when you decide you belong.”

## CTA
Comment “I BELONG.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
