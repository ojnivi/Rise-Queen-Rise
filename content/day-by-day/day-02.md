# Day 2 — THE COCOON SEASON
**Week:** Week 1 — Call & Cocoon

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman is wrapped inside a translucent purple silk cocoon suspended in a crystal garden. Golden light shines through the cocoon as purple and gold butterflies flutter around her, waiting for her transformation. Her silhouette inside the cocoon shows the beginning of a couture gown forming around her body. The atmosphere is sacred, luxurious, emotional, and magical, with gold dust, soft purple fog, crystal petals, and cinematic lighting.

## Voiceover
“Your cocoon was never your prison. It was your preparation. Every quiet season, every lonely night, every hidden tear was shaping wings the world had not yet seen.”

## CTA
Double-tap if you’re trusting your transformation.

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
