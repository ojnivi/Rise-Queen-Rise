# Day 19 — THE CROWN WITHIN
**Week:** Week 3 — Breakthrough

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman places her hand over her heart as a glowing golden crown radiates from within her chest. Purple and gold butterflies circle around her body and wings. She wears a fitted royal-purple couture gown with gold crystal ribbing and butterfly-wing sleeves. Inner sovereignty, emotional strength, luxury portrait, dramatic lighting.

## Voiceover
“The crown was never just above you. It was within you. Every time you chose healing, every time you chose truth, every time you chose yourself, it began to shine.”

## CTA
Comment “THE CROWN IS WITHIN.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
