# Day 9 — RELEASING FEAR
**Week:** Week 2 — Wings & Power

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman stands in a deep violet enchanted forest, opening her hands as dark smoke rises from her palms and transforms into purple and gold butterflies. She wears a sculptural purple gown with a molten gold bodice and dramatic butterfly-shaped skirt. The crown is still floating behind her, waiting. The mood is magical, emotional, powerful, and liberating.

## Voiceover
“Fear will always ask you to shrink. But your destiny requires expansion. Today, let fear leave your hands and become something beautiful.”

## CTA
Comment “FEAR DOES NOT OWN ME.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
