# Day 8 — WINGS BEGIN TO GROW
**Week:** Week 2 — Wings & Power

## Graphic Prompt
A close-up luxury portrait of an African American woman looking over her shoulder as delicate purple and gold butterfly wings begin growing from her back. She wears a gold-structured purple couture bodice with crystal details. Tiny purple and gold butterflies gather around the new wings like royal attendants. Her expression is surprised, emotional, and accepting. Editorial beauty campaign style, glowing skin, soft gold light.

## Voiceover
“Sometimes growth feels unfamiliar before it feels beautiful. Do not be afraid of the new strength forming in you. These wings were always yours.”

## CTA
Comment “MY WINGS ARE FORMING.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
