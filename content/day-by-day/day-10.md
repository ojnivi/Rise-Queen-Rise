# Day 10 — THE FIRST RISE
**Week:** Week 2 — Wings & Power

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman lifts slightly from the ground for the first time, her purple and gold butterfly wings half-open and glowing. Below her lies the broken cocoon on a bed of crystals and purple roses. Her couture gown flows downward like royal purple smoke threaded with gold. Purple and gold butterflies spiral upward around her. Epic luxury fantasy, cinematic ascension, emotional victory.

## Voiceover
“Look at you rising. Not because life was easy. Not because the road was clear. But because something inside you refused to stay down.”

## CTA
Comment “RISE QUEEN RISE.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
