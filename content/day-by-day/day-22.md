# Day 22 — LEARNING TO FLY
**Week:** Week 4 — Crown & Throne

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman runs across a glowing golden runway, wings open but still learning balance. Purple and gold butterflies fly beside her like a royal escort. Her purple gown has a detachable butterfly-wing train lifting behind her. She looks determined, joyful, and brave. High-fashion runway fantasy, motion, luxury, triumph.

## Voiceover
“You do not have to master flight on the first day. Wobble if you must. Run if you must. Crawl if you must. Just do not fold your wings out of fear.”

## CTA
Tag someone learning to fly again.

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
