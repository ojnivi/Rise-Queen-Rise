# Day 11 — THE REFLECTION OF POWER
**Week:** Week 2 — Wings & Power

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman kneels beside a golden reflecting pool. The water shows her future self as a crowned butterfly queen with full purple and gold wings. Around the pool, purple and gold butterflies skim the surface, leaving glowing trails. She wears a royal purple gown with gold crystal shoulder pieces and flowing silk sleeves. Serene, luxurious, deeply emotional.

## Voiceover
“Power does not always arrive as thunder. Sometimes it arrives as a whisper that says, ‘You are ready now.’ Listen closely. Your soul has been speaking.”

## CTA
Save this if you’re learning to trust your power.
