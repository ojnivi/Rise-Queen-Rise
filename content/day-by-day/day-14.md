# Day 14 — STANDING TALLER
**Week:** Week 2 — Wings & Power

## Graphic Prompt
Full-body editorial image of an African American woman standing on a marble pedestal, chin lifted, crown hovering above her head. She wears an architectural royal-purple gown with gold pleated butterfly-wing panels unfolding behind her. Purple and gold butterflies swirl around her like a royal storm. Dramatic spotlight, confident posture, couture opulence.

## Voiceover
“Every time you stopped apologizing for your light, you stood taller. Every time you chose yourself, your crown came closer.”

## CTA
Comment “I STAND TALL.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
