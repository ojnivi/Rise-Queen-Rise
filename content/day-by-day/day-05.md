# Day 5 — THE MIRROR OF BECOMING
**Week:** Week 1 — Call & Cocoon

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman stands before an enormous antique gold mirror. In the real world, she is still transforming, wearing a partially formed purple and gold couture gown. In the mirror reflection, she appears as a fully crowned queen with magnificent designer purple and gold butterfly wings. Purple and gold butterflies circle the mirror frame. The scene should feel emotional, luxurious, magical, and empowering.

## Voiceover
“The woman you are becoming already exists. She is waiting for you in your courage, in your discipline, in your healing, and in your decision to believe you are worthy.”

## CTA
Type “SHE’S COMING” if you’re becoming her.

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
