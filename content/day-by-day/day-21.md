# Day 21 — THE OLD WORLD FALLS AWAY
**Week:** Week 3 — Breakthrough

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman steps from a cracked gray city street into a glowing purple and gold palace realm. Behind her, the old world crumbles into purple and gold butterflies. Her gown transitions from simple fabric near the hem into full couture gold embroidery at the bodice. Her wings spread proudly behind her. Symbolic rebirth, luxury surrealism, cinematic motion.

## Voiceover
“You are not required to stay in rooms that only knew the smaller version of you. Let the old world fall away. Your new realm is waiting.”

## CTA
Comment “I’M ENTERING MY NEW REALM.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
