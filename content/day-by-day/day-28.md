# Day 28 — THE THRONE APPEARS
**Week:** Week 4 — Crown & Throne

## Graphic Prompt
A golden throne made entirely of butterfly wings forms behind an African American queen as she approaches. Purple and gold butterflies build the throne piece by piece in midair. She wears a purple and gold haute couture gown with a sweeping train, jeweled bodice, full butterfly wings, and radiant crown. Regal destiny, opulent palace, cinematic action.

## Voiceover
“The throne did not appear before the transformation. It appeared after she rose, after she released, after she remembered. Some seats are only revealed when you become ready to occupy them.”

## CTA
Comment “MY THRONE IS WAITING.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
