# Day 29 — SHE TAKES HER SEAT
**Week:** Week 4 — Crown & Throne

## Graphic Prompt
The crowned African American butterfly queen sits on a golden butterfly throne, radiant and calm. Her purple gown cascades down the throne steps like royal purple water threaded with molten gold. Purple and gold butterflies rest on her crown, shoulders, wings, throne, and train. Her expression is peaceful, powerful, and fully claimed. Luxury coronation portrait, divine royalty, magazine cover quality.

## Voiceover
“She no longer chases what is hers. She no longer begs the room to see her. She sits in her power, crowned by everything she survived.”

## CTA
Comment “I CLAIM MY SEAT.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
