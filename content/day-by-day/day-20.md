# Day 20 — FULL BREAKTHROUGH
**Week:** Week 3 — Breakthrough

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman bursts completely from a massive purple and gold cocoon. Silk fragments fly through the air like ribbons. Her full designer butterfly wings explode open behind her, jeweled and enormous. She wears an avant-garde royal purple and metallic gold gown with a cathedral train. Thousands of purple and gold butterflies pour from the cocoon. Epic transformation climax, cinematic luxury, powerful movement.

## Voiceover
“The cocoon can no longer contain what was placed inside you. This is your breakthrough. This is your emergence. This is the moment the world meets the woman you fought to become.”

## CTA
Comment “MY WINGS ARE READY.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
