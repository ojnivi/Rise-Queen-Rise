# Day 15 — THE BECOMING CEREMONY
**Week:** Week 3 — Breakthrough

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman stands in the center of a golden ceremonial circle surrounded by purple and gold butterflies forming a halo. Her gown is half simple purple fabric on one side and full haute couture royalty on the other, showing transformation in progress. One butterfly wing is fully grown, the other still forming. Sacred luxury ceremony, cinematic, emotional, magical realism.

## Voiceover
“You are allowed to be unfinished and powerful at the same time. Becoming is not weakness. Becoming is evidence that your next level is alive.”

## CTA
Comment “I’M STILL BECOMING.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
