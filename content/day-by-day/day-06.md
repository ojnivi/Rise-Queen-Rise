# Day 6 — THE FIRST STEP FORWARD
**Week:** Week 1 — Call & Cocoon

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman takes her first step out of a cracked cocoon shell. One foot is still near the old shell, the other steps onto a glowing gold path. She wears a dramatic purple silk gown with metallic gold corset details. Purple and gold butterflies rise from the broken cocoon behind her. Small luxury butterfly wings are beginning to unfold from her shoulders. Cinematic, couture, symbolic, triumphant.

## Voiceover
“The first step does not have to be loud. It only has to be honest. You do not need the whole path today. You only need the courage to move.”

## CTA
Comment “ONE STEP” if you’re moving forward.

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
