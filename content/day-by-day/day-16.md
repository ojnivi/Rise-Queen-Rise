# Day 16 — CUTTING INVISIBLE STRINGS
**Week:** Week 3 — Breakthrough

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman raises a gold jeweled fan shaped like a butterfly wing and cuts invisible strings pulling her backward. The strings snap and become purple and gold butterflies. She wears a bold purple haute couture gown with a huge gold collar and flowing butterfly-wing train. Her expression is fierce and peaceful. Dramatic action, regal editorial fantasy.

## Voiceover
“Some things held you without touching you. Old words. Old wounds. Old limits. Today, you cut the invisible strings and take your power back.”

## CTA
Comment “I CUT THE STRINGS.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
