# Day 26 — THE PAST BOWS
**Week:** Week 4 — Crown & Throne

## Graphic Prompt
The crowned African American queen walks forward while shadow versions of her former self bow respectfully behind her, dissolving into purple and gold butterflies. She does not look back. Her full wings are open, her crown is radiant, and her gown is a dramatic purple mermaid couture silhouette with gold sculptural butterfly appliqué. Regal release, closure, cinematic depth.

## Voiceover
“You do not have to hate who you used to be. She survived long enough to bring you here. Bless her. Release her. Then walk forward.”

## CTA
Comment “THANK YOU, OLD ME.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
