# Day 13 — SHATTERING THE CAGE
**Week:** Week 2 — Wings & Power

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman breaks through a golden cage made of old expectations. The cage bars crack and transform into purple and gold butterflies. She wears a dramatic purple ball gown with gold metallic armor at the waist and shoulders. Her butterfly wings burst wide behind her. Glass-like fragments, gold dust, luxury fantasy, powerful liberation scene.

## Voiceover
“You were not born to decorate a cage. You were born to break it, rise from it, and turn every broken bar into wings.”

## CTA
Comment “NO MORE CAGES.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
