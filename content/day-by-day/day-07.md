# Day 7 — THE CROWN IN THE DISTANCE
**Week:** Week 1 — Call & Cocoon

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman walks up a grand glowing staircase made of gold light. At the top, a jeweled crown floats beneath a storm of purple and gold butterflies. She wears an avant-garde purple gown with a long gold-embroidered train covered in butterfly details. Her posture is determined and graceful. The image should feel like ascension, destiny, luxury, and royal calling.

## Voiceover
“Your crown may look far away, but every healed wound, every brave choice, and every act of self-love is bringing you closer. Keep climbing, queen.”

## CTA
Tag a queen who needs to keep climbing.

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
