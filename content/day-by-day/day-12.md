# Day 12 — THE CROWN CHOOSES HER
**Week:** Week 2 — Wings & Power

## Graphic Prompt
A golden crown descends toward an African American woman standing on a circular gold platform. She wears a purple and gold avant-garde gown with butterfly-wing sleeves and crystal embroidery. Purple and gold butterflies gather beneath the crown as if carrying it down from heaven. Her arms are open, and her face shows surrender and strength. Sacred coronation energy, luxury, cinematic gold rays.

## Voiceover
“A crown is not given to make you worthy. It appears when you finally recognize that you already are. The crown did not create the queen. It confirmed her.”

## CTA
Drop a crown emoji if you’re claiming your worth.

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
