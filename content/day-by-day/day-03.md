# Day 3 — BREAKING THE FIRST THREAD
**Week:** Week 1 — Call & Cocoon

## Graphic Prompt
An African American woman begins tearing open a massive purple and gold cocoon with jeweled hands. Her avant-garde haute couture purple and gold gown is forming as silk wraps fall away. Purple and gold butterflies burst from the opening, surrounding her face and shoulders. Small butterfly wings begin growing from her back. The mood is powerful, emotional, and cinematic, showing the first act of choosing freedom.

## Voiceover
“You cannot become who you were born to be while holding on to who fear told you to remain. Today, one thread breaks. Tomorrow, the whole cocoon opens.”

## CTA
Comment “I’M BREAKING FREE.”

## Luxury Brand Watermark
Place “Lady Butterfly Designs” in elegant gold embossed calligraphy in the lower right corner, with a small jeweled purple and gold butterfly perched near the final letter. Sophisticated luxury fashion house branding, visible but not distracting.
