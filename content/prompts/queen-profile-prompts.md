## Brand rules (must keep)

- **Cast:** All characters Afro-American / Black — all shades welcome.
- **Age range:** Queens ~ages 25–55 (Legacy scenes may include older ancestors).
- **Butterfly:** Catalyst / symbol only. Prefer **NO wings on the queen’s body**. Couture is **architectural / Avagard**: rigid, sculptural, phi-spiral geometry — not silky flowing fabric — unless a prompt explicitly says otherwise.
- **Palette:** Purple + gold luxury **Lady Butterfly Designs** aesthetic.
- **Identity:** Different look per queen (shared brand DNA, not one identical face).
- **Motion (video variants):** Dress motion only when specified; do not move the woman unless asked.
- **Naming:** Worth is the foundation. Profiles are **emotional seasons**, not mythical names.

**Shared negative / avoid (append when helpful):** wings growing from her back or body; mythical/fantasy queen titles as labels; non-Black casts; silky flowing fairy gown unless intentional; soft stock-photo blandness; identical face reused across profiles.
