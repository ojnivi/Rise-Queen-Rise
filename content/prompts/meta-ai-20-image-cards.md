## Brand rules (must keep)

- **Cast:** Afro-American / Black queens — all shades welcome; ages ~25–55.
- **Butterfly:** Symbol / motif on the card (frame, embossing, orbiting accent). Prefer **no wings fused to her body** unless an elegant motif detail is intentional.
- **Couture:** If a queen figure appears — architectural / sculptural Avagard couture (phi-spiral geometry, structured panels), not soft fairy silk.
- **Palette:** Purple `#5B2C83` + gold `#C9A86A`; embossed gold frame; spirals, laurels, butterflies, crowns as brand ornaments.
- **Identity:** Emotional / empowerment cards — **no mythical queen title characters**.
- **Watermark note:** Soft corner credit *Lady Butterfly Designs* (or space for it in Canva).
- **Format:** Vertical luxury **image card** (9:16 or square card feel), embossed royalty stationery look, readable on-card typography.

**Shared avoid (append when helpful):** wings growing from her back; mythical/fantasy queen labels; non-Black cast; soft stock blandness; cluttered unreadable text; identical face reused across cards.

**How to use:** Replace `[brackets]` with her name, story, dates, or specifics before generating. Keep brand palette and embossed-card formula.
