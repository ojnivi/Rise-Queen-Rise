# 📁 PHI Folder

This folder contains **Personal Health Information** and wellness-related content for the Rise Queen Rise community.

## 🏥 What's Inside

This folder is dedicated to resources that support:
- 💪 **Physical wellness** - Fitness tips, health articles, wellness routines
- 🧠 **Mental health** - Mental wellness resources, coping strategies, mindfulness guides
- 💖 **Emotional wellness** - Emotional intelligence, self-care practices, healing content
- 🌱 **Holistic health** - Nutrition tips, sleep hygiene, stress management
- 🧘 **Mindfulness & meditation** - Guided practices, breathing exercises, meditation resources

## 📂 Subfolder Structure

- **mental-health/** - Mental wellness articles and resources
- **physical-wellness/** - Fitness and physical health content
- **emotional-care/** - Self-care guides and emotional support resources
- **nutrition/** - Healthy eating guides and recipes
- **meditation-mindfulness/** - Guided practices and mindfulness resources
- **sleep-wellness/** - Sleep hygiene tips and rest guides
- **wellness-challenges/** - Community wellness challenges and goals

## 🤝 Contributing to PHI

We welcome contributions that help our community thrive! Please:

1. **Be evidence-based** - Share credible health information
2. **Include disclaimers** - Note that content is informational, not medical advice
3. **Be inclusive** - Consider different abilities, backgrounds, and lifestyles
4. **Add citations** - Link to sources when appropriate
5. **Be compassionate** - Remember these are sensitive topics

## ⚠️ Important Note

**This content is for informational purposes only.** We are not healthcare professionals. If you're struggling with serious mental or physical health issues, please reach out to a qualified healthcare provider.
