# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Rise Queen Rise is dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all members of our community. We are committed to creating a space where every queen can rise, shine, and thrive without fear of discrimination, harassment, or disrespect.

## Our Values

- **Respect** - We treat all members with dignity and respect
- **Inclusivity** - We welcome people of all backgrounds, identities, and experiences
- **Empowerment** - We lift each other up and celebrate each other's growth
- **Authenticity** - We encourage genuine self-expression
- **Kindness** - We practice compassion and understanding
- **Accountability** - We take responsibility for our actions

## Expected Behavior

Members of the Rise Queen Rise community are expected to:

- **Be respectful** - Use inclusive language and respect diverse perspectives
- **Be supportive** - Encourage and uplift others in their personal growth
- **Be constructive** - Provide feedback that is helpful and kind
- **Be authentic** - Share your true self while respecting others' boundaries
- **Be accountable** - Take responsibility for your words and actions
- **Be patient** - Remember that we're all on different journeys
- **Be open-minded** - Listen to understand, not just to respond

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any kind
- Discrimination based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other identity
- Hate speech, slurs, or derogatory language
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate exclusion or isolation of community members
- Sharing of private information without consent
- Spamming, trolling, or intentionally disruptive behavior
- Any form of violence or threats
- Abuse of power or authority

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

1. **Document** - Note the incident (date, time, what happened)
2. **Report** - Contact the project maintainers immediately
3. **Provide Details** - Share as much information as you're comfortable with
4. **Confidentiality** - Your report will be handled with discretion

**How to Report:**
- Open an issue marked as "Code of Conduct Violation"
- Email the project maintainers directly
- Use private messages through GitHub

## Enforcement

The Rise Queen Rise team will:

1. Review all reports promptly and fairly
2. Investigate the incident thoroughly
3. Maintain confidentiality during the process
4. Take appropriate action based on the findings

**Possible Actions Include:**
- A private conversation with the person in question
- A public apology or clarification
- Temporary or permanent removal from the community
- Other remedies deemed appropriate

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All spaces within this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Official community events and meetings
- Communications related to Rise Queen Rise
- Any representation of the community

## Questions?

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct, please don't hesitate to ask. We're here to support you and ensure this is a safe, welcoming space for all.

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is inspired by best practices in open-source communities and is designed to reflect the values of Rise Queen Rise.
