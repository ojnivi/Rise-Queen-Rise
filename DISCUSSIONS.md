# GitHub Discussions Guide

Welcome to Rise Queen Rise Discussions! 👑

This is our community forum where queens can connect, share ideas, ask questions, and support each other. It's a space for meaningful conversation beyond just code and content submissions.

## 📚 Discussion Categories

### 💡 Ideas
Share and discuss new ideas for Rise Queen Rise:
- Suggest new content themes
- Propose new features or improvements
- Discuss how we can better serve the community
- Share creative inspiration

**Use this for:** Brainstorming, suggestions, feature ideas

---

### ❓ Q&A
Ask questions and get answers from the community:
- How do I use Rise Queen Rise?
- Where can I find specific content?
- How do I contribute?
- General questions about the project

**Use this for:** Questions, troubleshooting, guidance

---

### 📣 Announcements
Stay updated on important news:
- Major updates and releases
- New content collections
- Community milestones
- Event announcements

**Use this for:** Official announcements, news, updates

---

### 💬 General
General discussion and community connection:
- Share your personal stories and experiences
- Celebrate victories and milestones
- Connect with other queens
- Off-topic conversations (still respectful!)

**Use this for:** Community building, general chat, support

---

### 🎉 Show & Tell
Showcase what you've created or discovered:
- Share how you've used Rise Queen Rise content
- Show results or transformations
- Highlight your favorite resources
- Feature community creations

**Use this for:** Sharing accomplishments, featuring community work

---

## 🌟 How to Start a Discussion

1. **Go to Discussions** - Click the "Discussions" tab at the top of the repository
2. **Click "New Discussion"** - Start a fresh conversation
3. **Choose a Category** - Select the most relevant category
4. **Write a Title** - Make it clear and descriptive
5. **Add Details** - Provide context and information
6. **Post** - Click "Create Discussion"

---

## 💬 Commenting & Voting

- **Reply** - Add your thoughts to any discussion
- **👍 Vote** - Use reactions to show support
- **📌 Pin** - Maintainers can pin important discussions
- **✅ Mark as Answer** - Help others by marking helpful responses

---

## 📋 Discussion Guidelines

### ✅ DO:
- Be respectful and kind
- Stay on topic
- Provide context and details
- Search before posting duplicates
- Use appropriate category
- Follow the Code of Conduct
- Ask clarifying questions
- Celebrate others' contributions

### ❌ DON'T:
- Post spam or promotional content
- Use offensive language
- Derail conversations
- Cross-post the same discussion multiple times
- Share private information
- Be dismissive or rude
- Advertise unrelated products/services

---

## 🎯 Tips for Great Discussions

### For Question Askers:
1. **Be Specific** - Include details and context
2. **Search First** - Check if your question has been answered
3. **Try First** - Share what you've already tried
4. **Be Patient** - Give people time to respond
5. **Say Thanks** - Acknowledge helpful responses

### For Question Answerers:
1. **Be Helpful** - Provide clear, useful information
2. **Be Kind** - Remember you're helping someone learn
3. **Add Links** - Share relevant resources
4. **Ask Follow-ups** - Help clarify what they need
5. **Mark Solutions** - Help others find answers

---

## 🔍 Searching Discussions

Can't find what you're looking for?

1. **Use Search Bar** - Type keywords at the top
2. **Filter by Category** - Click category names
3. **Sort by Date** - Find newest or most active
4. **Check Unanswered** - Find discussions needing help

---

## 🚀 Best Practices

**Starting Strong:**
- Write a clear, specific title
- Use the description to provide context
- Include relevant links or references
- Be friendly and welcoming

**Keeping Discussions Active:**
- Reply to comments thoughtfully
- Ask follow-up questions
- Share resources and knowledge
- Celebrate community contributions

**Closing Out:**
- Mark helpful answers
- Thank contributors
- Summarize learnings if helpful
- Consider sharing insights as a discussion reply

---

## 🤝 Community Moderation

Our maintainers will:
- Monitor discussions for Code of Conduct violations
- Move off-topic conversations to appropriate channels
- Pin important discussions
- Mark answered questions
- Archive inactive discussions after 6 months

---

## 📞 When to Use Discussions vs Issues

| Use Discussions For: | Use Issues For: |
|---|---|
| Asking questions | Reporting bugs |
| Sharing ideas | Requesting specific features |
| General conversation | Tracking work to be done |
| Announcements | Documenting problems |
| Community building | Action items |

## 🎓 FAQ

**Q: Will my discussion get a response?**
A: We try to respond to all discussions, but response time varies. Be patient and help others too!

**Q: Can I delete my discussion?**
A: You can delete your own discussions, but we prefer to keep them for community benefit.

**Q: How do I report inappropriate content?**
A: Flag the discussion/comment to maintainers or email directly. We take Code of Conduct violations seriously.

**Q: Can I share external links?**
A: Yes, if they're relevant and helpful. No spam or self-promotion without prior approval.
